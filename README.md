# ⚽ FIFA 20 Player Analysis & Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the FIFA 20 player dataset to uncover valuable insights about player attributes, overall ratings, potential ratings, wages, market values, and performance trends.

The objective is to analyze player characteristics, identify top-performing players, understand relationships between attributes, and derive actionable insights from the dataset using Python and data visualization techniques.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze FIFA 20 player data.
- Understand player performance distribution.
- Identify top-rated and high-potential players.
- Explore relationships between player attributes.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Generate meaningful visualizations and business insights.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains information about FIFA 20 players, including:

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🔹 Player Ratings
- Most players have overall ratings between 60 and 75.
- Only a small percentage of players achieve elite ratings above 85.

### 🔹 Age Analysis
- Peak-performing players are generally between 25 and 30 years old.
- Younger players tend to have higher potential ratings.

### 🔹 Market Value
- Players with higher overall ratings command significantly greater market values.
- Market value increases with player performance and potential.

### 🔹 Potential Players
- Several young players show high potential, making them valuable future investments.

### 🔹 Correlation Findings
- Overall rating strongly correlates with technical and physical attributes.
- Potential rating has a positive relationship with market value.
